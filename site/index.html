06 - The guarantee Four clauses, not four slogans

This program never deletes a photo, never touches what you discard, never overwrites a file and never uploads anything, anywhere.

01

No delete exists

There’s no trash and no “are you sure”: the action simply isn’t in the app.

02

Discards stay put

Not moved, not tagged, not renamed. A discard is a decision, not an edit.

03

Nothing overwritten

A taken name lands beside the original, never on top of it. Run the transfer twice and you still lose nothing.

IMG_0412_1.JPG

04

It stays on your machine

Bound to 127.0.0.1, with no option to change it. No account, no cloud, no internet.

07 - The fine print Including the parts we’d rather not mention

Resumes mid-roll

Decisions are keyed to file names, so you can review several folders, quit, and pick each one up where you left it.

Copy or move

Copy leaves the source folder complete so you can check the result first. Move leaves behind exactly what you discarded.

RAW pairing, optional

CR2, NEF, ARW, DNG and friends follow the photo that shares their name. One switch turns it off.

Top level only

Subfolders aren’t searched and their photos never enter the queue. Point it at the folder that actually holds the shoot.

Undo, not rewrite

Undo takes back the most recent call. Changing an older verdict means undoing your way back to it.

No edits, no ratings

Videos are ignored, stars don’t exist and nothing gets developed here. It does one job and then gets out of the way.
